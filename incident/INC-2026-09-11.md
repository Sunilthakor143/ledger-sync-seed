# INC-2026-09-11 — Operational Incident Summary

1. WHAT BROKE: Account 4821 displayed a false ₹92,213.10 debit for a ₹5.00 water can purchase due to balance string mis-parsing.
2. HOW FOUND: Discovered via customer complaint and automated alert `ledger.balance.divergence` reporting a balance divergence on account 4821.
3. WHO AFFECTED: Customers receiving bank notifications where whole-rupee transaction amounts were followed by stated account balances.
4. ROOT CAUSE: `Amounts.extractFirstAmount()` matched the first numeric token (stated balance) instead of skipping balance prefixes when amount lacked decimals.
5. PREVENTION: `Amounts.java` was updated to prioritize explicit transaction amount tokens before balance markers, backed by `AmountsTest` regression suite.
